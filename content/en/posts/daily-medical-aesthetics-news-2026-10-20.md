Traditional topical collagen cannot penetrate the stratum corneum barrier (exceeding the 500-Dalton limit); mechanical microneedling or conventional mesotherapy inflict puncture trauma, bleeding, downtime, and irregular drug distribution. ## II. Ultrafast Femtosecond Laser-Induced Optical Breakdown (FL-LIOB) with Spatial Light Modulator (SLM) Multifocal Microcavitation: Pure Dermal Extracellular Matrix Remodeling without Epidermal Disruption for Atrophic Acne Scars
